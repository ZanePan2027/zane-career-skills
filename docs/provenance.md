# 来源与许可证 / Provenance and license

走出象牙塔由 Zane 维护，方法来自简历、双语作品集、案例叙事、视觉设计与求职交付实践。

Beyond the Ivory Tower is maintained by Zane. Its methods grew from practical work on resumes, bilingual portfolios, project stories, visual design, and application materials.

## 设计参考 / Design references

- [dontbesilent2025/dbskill](https://github.com/dontbesilent2025/dbskill)：参考按任务选方法、从来源取材、用对照检查判断的设计方式。本版校园与面试例子由 Zane 编写。Informed task selection, source-based material gathering, and contrasts used to examine a judgment. The student and interview examples were written for this toolkit.
- [NACE, Job Outlook 2026](https://www.naceweb.org/about-us/press/2026/how-new-grad-job-seekers-can-stand-out-with-potential-employers)：关于将课程与活动经历表达为岗位技能的雇主调查材料。提供美国招聘语境参考。Employer survey material on articulating skills from coursework and activities, in the US hiring context.
